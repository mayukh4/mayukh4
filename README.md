## 🔭 Research

My PhD is in high-frequency radio instrumentation: balloon-borne VLBI, FPGA digital backends, and black hole imaging.

**BVEX — Balloon-borne VLBI Experiment.** A K-band radio telescope on a stratospheric balloon, observing bright radio galaxies together with ground arrays like the VLBA. It flew from Timmins, Ontario in August 2025 on the CSA STRATOS campaign. I work on the flight software, the ground station and the digital backend.

| Project | What it is |
|---|---|
| [**fissellab/bcp**](https://github.com/fissellab/bcp) | BVEX flight control program (C) |
| [**fissellab/bvex_gs**](https://github.com/fissellab/bvex_gs) | Ground station GUI for live payload telemetry |
| [**bvex-vdif-rfsoc**](https://github.com/mayukh4/bvex-vdif-rfsoc) | RFSoC 4x2 VDIF packetizer + AF_XDP no-drop recorder: 2-bit I/Q at ~8.3 Gbps over 100GbE, straight into DiFX |
| [**BVEX_rfsoc_4x2**](https://github.com/mayukh4/BVEX_rfsoc_4x2) | CASPER designs for the BVEX backend: 120 kHz water-maser spectrometer and VLBI acquisition |
| [**ngehtsim**](https://github.com/mayukh4/ngehtsim) · [**eht-imaging**](https://github.com/mayukh4/eht-imaging) | Simulations for ngEHT and space/balloon VLBI of black hole photon rings |
| [**CRASIES_wok_telescope**](https://github.com/mayukh4/CRASIES_wok_telescope) | A 21 cm hydrogen-line telescope made from a wok, for summer-school labs |

Earlier: CCAT-prime [atmospheric modelling](https://github.com/mayukh4/CCAT-P-Atmospheric-Modelling) and [KID resonator tracking](https://github.com/ccat850/resonator_tracking), plus [dust polarization](https://github.com/mayukh4/Dust_polarization_data) analysis.

## 🛠️ Builds

On the side I build local-first AI hardware: things that run on a Pi or an old phone, with no cloud and no account. Most of them have a video on [Mayukh Builds](https://www.youtube.com/@MayukhBuilds).

| Project | What it is |
|---|---|
| [**linux-android**](https://github.com/mayukh4/linux-android) ![stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/mayukh4/linux-android?style=flat&label=★) | Turn an old Android phone into a GPU-accelerated Linux desktop or Home Assistant server. No root, no PC |
| [**jansky**](https://github.com/mayukh4/jansky) | Always-on voice agent on a Pi 5 + AI HAT+ 2: Qwen3 on the Hailo-10H, wake word, Home Assistant, memory, a face |
| [**perch**](https://github.com/mayukh4/perch) | A Raspberry Pi desk dashboard you can talk to: widgets, touch and a voice button wired to any AI |
| [**openclaw-command-center**](https://github.com/mayukh4/openclaw-command-center) | A 24/7 voice + touch mission control for OpenClaw agents |
| [**desk-buddy**](https://github.com/mayukh4/desk-buddy) | Palm-sized desk robot, fully on-device: local ASR, llama.cpp, Piper TTS, NPU vision, ROS2 |
| [**unihiker-offline-ai**](https://github.com/mayukh4/unihiker-offline-ai) | An LLM running offline on a $40 board, benchmarked against a Pi 5 and a $1000 mini PC |
